svgbob drawer

Shapes you draw are written out as svgbob ASCII in the text box. You can also type straight into the text box. Characters you add there, or import or paste, are loose characters: they show in a warmer colour and can be box-selected and moved like shapes.

Diamond (wider makes a hexagon)

Text

Eraser (Alt punches single characters)

Colours

Solid boxes and circles can take a fill and outline colour. The app writes svgbob's {tag} inside the shape and a # Legend: at the bottom of the text. svgbob ignores the colour if a line joins the shape's edge, or if there's no room inside for the tag.
